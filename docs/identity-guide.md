# Byturn identity guide / v1

Approved: name, symbol, custom lettering, Outfit, palette and lockup proportion. Usage rules and minimum-size recommendations are proposals for owner review.

## Keep the master geometry.

Use the supplied vector. The wordmark keeps the straight y–t connection and curved t–u connection. The symbol is approximately the visible height of the lowercase n (ratio 1.032), not the full ascender height.

## Clear space & minimum sizes

Proposed rule: use half the visible n height as one clear-space unit around the lockup. Start at 135 px wide for the full signature and 16 px for the standalone symbol; prefer 180 px and 24 px in working interfaces. Verify optical legibility at the final export size. These minimums are usage proposals for review.

## What changes the mark

Do not redraw the letters, separate the joined strokes, enlarge the symbol to the ascenders, stretch the lockup, or place it on a busy image without a quiet surface. Misuse examples below are illustrations, not alternative assets.

## Confident, clear, collaborative.

Name the action and the version. Explain what happens next. Keep errors useful and recoverable. Avoid blame, exaggerated celebration and vague “Done” messages for irreversible decisions.

## Outfit. With a custom signature.

Outfit 400 / 500 / 600 supports the interface. The approved wordmark is a vector drawing; typing “byturn” in Outfit does not reproduce the custom lettering.

## One icon family / Phosphor

Regular-weight Phosphor icons, 16 px in standard controls. Pair unfamiliar actions with text. Icon-only buttons require an accessible name; decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology.

## The identity, in use.

An announcement, a presentation cover and a delivery card. The mark leads; the message gives it context.

The approved lockup stays centered on both axes. A small upper label and a separate lower message frame the mark without shifting its center. Short copy stays inside generous margins. Orbit colors remain transparent at 34% over the dark surface and stay away from the logo. These are composition studies, not new logo or palette proposals.

## Color

Electric yellow #F2FF40. Orbit 02: blue #369CFF, violet #AD64FF, pink #FF6DB7. Dark: normal alpha 34%; light: multiply alpha 24%. Text stays above the tint. Preserve these transparent layers. Semantic decision colors are separate.

## Export use

Use master SVGs rather than reconstructing the logo from font text. Ink, paper and yellow SVG/PNG variants preserve the same paths. The symbol-to-lowercase-n visible height ratio is approximately 1.032. The standalone symbol is contextual; primary signatures should use the approved lockup. PNG exports have transparent backgrounds.
