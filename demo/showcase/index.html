English transcript

A poster can go through several rounds before it’s ready. Byturn keeps the artwork, feedback, and approval together.

The studio uploads a version and shares a review link. Clients can open it without creating an account.

Pin a comment to the exact spot that needs attention. Each note stays with its image and version.

When the revised artwork is ready, approve that version. The review locks, and the original stays available to download.
